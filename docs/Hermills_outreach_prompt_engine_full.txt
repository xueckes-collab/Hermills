1. OUTREACH ENGINE RUNTIME CHAIN
============================================================

The full outreach generation flow is:

1. Customer website research
   - Quick / balanced / deep / adaptive research.
   - Uses local Node fallback and, when available, deep research sidecar with Scrapling/Playwright.
   - Produces CustomerResearchResult:
     website, companyName, title, description, buyerType, industry, inferredNeed,
     productSignals, buyingSignals, painSignals, recommendedAngle, fetchedUrls,
     evidence, textPreview, error.

2. Company knowledge loading
   - Reads saved company profile and materials.
   - Extracts seller facts:
     Main products
     Certifications
     Payment terms
     Shipping terms
     Brand voice

3. Evidence and strategy map
   - Builds Outreach OS evidence map:
     VERIFIED facts
     INFERRED insights
     GENERIC context
     PROHIBITED claims
     Missing fields
   - Selects buyer reason, buyer segment, likely pain, procurement trigger,
     one USP, and one low-friction CTA.

4. Prompt generation
   Depending on the UI action:
   - Single draft: outreachInstructions + buildOutreachPrompt
   - Full workflow: outreachWorkflowInstructions + buildOutreachWorkflowPrompt
   - Manual rewrite: outreachInstructions + buildOutreachRewritePrompt
   - Automatic QA repair: outreachInstructions + buildOutreachRepairPrompt

5. LLM generation
   - Calls runtime.createHermesReply(...)
   - For OpenAI-compatible providers, calls:
     POST {baseUrl}/v1/chat/completions
   - The model is:
     request.model OR provider.defaultModel OR hermes-agent

6. JSON parsing
   - Single email output must parse as:
     {"subject":"...","body":"..."}
   - Workflow output must parse as:
     {
       "icps": [...],
       "usps": [...],
       "initialEmail": {"subject":"...","body":"..."},
       "followUps": [...]
     }

7. Critic / quality gate
   - Runs code-based checks.
   - If failed, automatic repair runs up to 2 times.
   - If still failed, fallback draft may be used if it scores better.

8. Send risk review
   - Blocks or warns before sending based on quality, claims, sender status,
     compliance, CTA asset availability, and unsubscribe presence.

14. EVIDENCE SELECTION AND STRATEGY LOGIC
Sources: strongestResearchSignal, bestCustomerResearchClues, selectOutreachUsp, selectMicroOffer
============================================================

Buyer reason selection order:
1. Use bestCustomerResearchClues(research, 2) when possible.
2. Use strongest non-inferred ranked website evidence.
3. Fall back through:
   buyingSignals[0]
   painSignals[0]
   inferredNeed
   recommendedAngle
   combinedResearchSignal
   productSignals[0]
   description
4. If all missing:
   lead.need OR lead.notes OR "appears to be reviewing {product}"

Concrete clue scoring rewards:
- program, range, collection, series, line, catalog, dealer, distributor,
  retailer, showroom, inventory, stock, quick-ship, truckload, container,
  direct, import, wholesale, contractor, commercial, residential.
- SPC, LVT, luxury vinyl, vinyl plank, rigid core, flooring, Fortika, OEM,
  private label.
- Specs such as mm, mil, inch, cm, wear layer, attached pad, waterproof,
  warranty, certification, specs.
- Proper-name phrases.

Concrete clue scoring penalizes:
- page title, meta description, lead website, generic sourcing context.
- appears, seems, likely, may, might, probably, inferred.
- skip to content, trusted manufacturer, flooring excellence, all rights reserved,
  privacy policy, terms of service.
- plain domains like example.com.

USP selection:
- If seller certifications exist and likely pain mentions proof/certification/quality/compliance/testing:
  headline: "{product} proof pack"
  buyerAngle: "Reduces buyer risk before sampling by putting certification or inspection evidence in the first review."
  proof: saved certifications

- If seller shipping terms exist and likely pain mentions lead time/delivery/logistics/supply/repeat/replenishment:
  headline: "Clear supply and delivery comparison"
  buyerAngle: "Helps the buyer compare lead time, MOQ, and shipment terms before committing to a long supplier conversation."
  proof: saved shipping terms

- If buyer segment is retail/ecommerce/brand/category:
  headline: "Category-fit {product} options"
  buyerAngle: "Gives the buyer a small set of options matched to their channel instead of a broad catalog."
  proof: available product line or proof-needed note

- Default:
  headline: "{product} backup options" or "Matched backup options"
  buyerAngle: "Lets the buyer compare specs, MOQ, lead time, and proof notes before adding a backup supplier."
  proof: saved product/certification info or proof-needed note

Micro-offer selection:
- Certification/proof pain:
  "a short certification/spec pack plus 2 matched options"
- Lead time/delivery/logistics/replenishment pain:
  "a small MOQ and lead-time comparison for 2-3 options"
- Catalog/range/SKU/category pain:
  "2-3 {product} options matched to their channel"
- Default:
  "2-3 matched options with MOQ, lead time, and proof notes"

Missing evidence list:
- certifications or compliance proof
- lead-time or shipping terms
- payment terms
- specific product list

   Label: Buyer-specific first line
   Pass message: The opening explains why this buyer is being contacted.
   Fail message: The first line does not clearly say why this buyer should care.

2. humanTone
   Label: Human English
   Pass message: The wording avoids obvious translated-template phrases.
   Fail message: Template phrase found: {template hit / stiff AI opening / translated sales wording}

3. personalized
   Label: Evidence-backed personalization
   Pass message: The message links a concrete buyer signal to a likely sourcing implication.
   Fail message: The message needs 2 concrete buyer clues plus a business implication, not just a name or category.

4. nextStep
   Label: Clear next step
   Pass message: The buyer can answer a low-friction micro-offer.
   Fail message: The CTA is too vague; offer a specific yes/no, A/B option, comparison, option list, specs, proof pack, or MOQ/lead-time table.

5. twoSecondRead
   Label: 2-second scan
   Pass message: The email is short enough to scan quickly.
   Fail message: Keep it like a human note: 35-110 words, short subject, 1-4 short paragraphs, no visible framework wording.

Hard pass conditions:
- score >= 90
- no hard failure among:
  buyerReasonPassed
  humanTonePassed
  personalizedPassed
  nextStepPassed
  twoSecondPassed

- Opening must exist.

Personalization requires:

- Does not look like a mass template.

2-second read requires:
- 35-110 words.

- Subject <= 50 characters.

  line 1, line 2, line 3, buyer implication, procurement trigger,

Rewrite hints generated by critic:
- Start with one specific product, channel, procurement, certification, project, or market clue from the buyer website; do not use only a domain, page title, or phrase like 'works around'.
- Remove translated-template phrases and write like a short human business note.
- Add at least two customer-specific clues from the buyer website and explain the buyer implication.
- End with one natural low-friction micro-offer: 2-3 matched options, an MOQ/lead-time table, a spec/certification pack, a short comparison, or a natural A/B choice. Avoid keyword-reply CTAs like Reply 'SPC table'.
- Rewrite as a 45-90 word human sales note, not a visible three-line formula.

- sender_disabled:
  Sender account is disabled.

- mailbox_not_confirmed:
  Sender mailbox must be tested and delivery-confirmed before sending.

- sender_identity:
  Sender email is invalid.

- lead_unsubscribed:
  This lead is marked unsubscribed.

- domain_alignment:

  CTA mentions a concrete asset but no matching saved asset/profile proof was found.

- unsubscribe_missing:
  No unsubscribe or opt-out sentence was found. Add one for cold outreach compliance where required.

Info:
- sender_not_checked:
  Sender account was not supplied for this draft risk check.

Score:
- Starts at 100.
- Each blocking issue subtracts 45.
- Each warning subtracts 10.
- Passed only when no blocking issues exist.
